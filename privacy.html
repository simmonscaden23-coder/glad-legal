Privacy Policy for Glad

Last updated: July 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Glad mobile app (“Glad”, “the app”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use it. By using Glad, you agree to the practices described here.

1. The short version

Glad does not have user accounts and does not ask you to sign in. Your missions, goals, chosen name, personality setting, and streak are stored only on your device. We do not operate a server that collects or stores your personal data. The limited exceptions — sending text to an AI service to generate messages, processing subscriptions, and using an advertising and analytics service to measure our marketing — are described below. On iOS, advertising measurement only happens if you allow it through Apple’s tracking prompt (see Section 6).

2. Information stored on your device

The following is saved locally on your phone (using the device’s on-device storage) and is not transmitted to us:

  • The name you choose to be called
  • Your missions and goals (titles, days, and times)
  • Your selected AI personality
  • Your completion streak and progress

If you delete the app, this information is erased from your device.

3. AI-generated messages

To create personalized reminders and encouragement in your chosen personality, Glad sends limited text to an AI processing service. Specifically, when a message is generated, the following may be transmitted: your chosen name, the title and time of the relevant mission or goal, and the selected personality style.

This text is sent over a secure connection to our processing endpoint, which forwards it to Google’s Gemini AI service to generate the response. We do not use this content to build a profile of you, and we do not sell it. Google’s handling of data sent to its AI services is governed by Google’s own privacy terms. We recommend you avoid putting sensitive personal information in mission or goal titles.

If the AI service is unavailable, Glad falls back to built-in messages and sends nothing.

4. Subscriptions and purchases

Glad is offered as an auto-renewing subscription that begins with a free trial. Purchases are processed by Apple and managed on our behalf by RevenueCat, our subscription infrastructure provider. To validate and restore purchases, RevenueCat processes purchase transaction information and an anonymous app user identifier. We never receive or store your credit card or full payment details — Apple handles all billing.

  • RevenueCat privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Apple’s privacy practices: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

5. Notifications

Glad sends local notifications (reminders) that are scheduled and delivered on your device. These do not transmit data to us. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device Settings.

6. Advertising and analytics (Meta)

To measure how well our advertising works and to understand how the app is installed and used, Glad uses the Meta (Facebook) SDK from Meta Platforms, Inc. When enabled, the Meta SDK may collect and share the following with Meta:

  • Your device’s advertising identifier (on iOS, the IDFA) and similar device or technical identifiers;
  • Standard app-activity events — such as installing the app, opening it, completing initial setup (a “registration”), and making a subscription purchase (including the purchase amount and currency);
  • Basic device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, and app version.

Meta uses this information to attribute app installs and events to our ads, to measure and improve ad performance, and — subject to your choices and Meta’s own policies — for Meta’s advertising and analytics. This means a limited amount of your app activity may be shared with a third party (Meta) for advertising measurement. Meta’s handling of this data is governed by Meta’s Privacy Policy: https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/.

Your choices:

  • On iOS, Glad will not use your advertising identifier for tracking unless you allow it through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track,” we do not share your advertising identifier with Meta. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • You can further limit ad tracking in your device settings and manage your ad preferences directly in your Meta/Facebook account.

7. Information we do NOT collect

  • We do not require an account and do not collect your name, email, or contacts for our own use.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • Aside from the advertising and analytics described in Section 6 — which on iOS requires your consent — we do not track you across other companies’ apps and websites.
  • We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

8. Data retention and deletion

Because your data lives on your device, you control it. To delete everything, delete the app. Text sent for AI generation is processed transiently to return a message and is not retained by us. Advertising and analytics events shared with Meta are retained and handled according to Meta’s policies; you can adjust or reset your advertising identifier in your device settings.

9. Children’s privacy

Glad is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will address it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above reflects the most recent version. Continued use of Glad after changes means you accept the updated policy.
